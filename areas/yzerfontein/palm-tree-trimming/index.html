How we can help

Palms need a different approach from broadleaf trees. Common landscape palms rely on a vital growing point at the crown, so topping is not a safe way to reduce height or reshape the plant.

  • Dead fronds create a falling risk or untidy skirt
  • Flowers or fruit drop onto paving, pools or vehicles
  • Old growth attracts pests or creates a fire concern
  • Fronds interfere with a building, access route or view
  • A palm needs routine maintenance rather than removal

Palm Tree Trimming considerations in Yzerfontein

  • Strong coastal exposure can affect tree shape, dead growth and the control needed during removal.
  • Holiday-home layouts, boundary walls and open or sandy ground can influence loading.
  • Wind-shaped trees and natural vegetation should be assessed for condition and appropriate retention.

For palm trimming, height, wind exposure and anything beneath the crown determine how fronds should be controlled and lowered.
