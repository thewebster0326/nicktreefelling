Safe, controlled tree felling for gardens, homes, businesses and open properties across the Western Cape.

Tree felling is sometimes the only practical option when a tree is hazardous, unwanted, poorly positioned or affecting the way a property can be used. Nick Tree Felling assesses the tree and its surroundings before deciding how it should be brought down.

Where space allows, a tree may be felled in a controlled direction. In confined gardens or near buildings, sectional dismantling and controlled lowering may be more appropriate. The method follows the site—not a one-size-fits-all price or approach.

When to call us

  • A dead, damaged or declining tree presents a safety concern
  • A tree stands too close to a house, wall, driveway or planned building
  • Roots or excessive growth are affecting how the property is used
  • An unwanted tree needs to be removed as part of landscaping
  • A large tree requires sectional dismantling in a restricted garden

Our approach

  1. Inspect the tree, its condition and the available working area
  2. Identify buildings, cables, plants and other features that need protection
  3. Choose open felling or sectional dismantling as conditions allow
  4. Section the timber and agree on stacking, removal and final cleanup

What your quote can include

  • Initial tree and site assessment
  • Controlled felling or sectional dismantling
  • Branch removal and trunk sectioning
  • Optional stump work by agreement
  • Optional timber and refuse removal
  • Final work-area cleanup as specified in the quote

What affects the cost?

Every property is different, so we quote for the actual work rather than promising a generic price. The main considerations include:

  • Tree height, trunk diameter and crown spread
  • Tree condition and structural stability
  • Access and distance to the loading area
  • Nearby structures, cables and rigging requirements
  • Stump work, disposal and the final cleanup scope

Frequently asked questions

Do you remove large trees?

Yes. Large-tree work is assessed individually so the cutting method, access and nearby risks can be planned properly.

Is stump removal included in tree felling?

It can be added to the scope, but it is not automatically included in every quote. Confirm whether you want the stump left, ground or removed.

Can you work close to a house?

Trees near buildings can often be dismantled in sections rather than felled whole. A site assessment determines the appropriate method.

Do you remove the branches and timber?

Removal is available and should be specified in your quote. You can also discuss leaving usable timber by prior agreement.
