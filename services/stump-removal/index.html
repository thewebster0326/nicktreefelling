Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between stump grinding and removal?

Grinding reduces the stump below the surrounding surface, while extraction removes more of the stump and root mass. The best option depends on access and future plans for the area.

Will stump removal damage my garden?

Some disturbance is normal, especially during extraction. Access and the work area should be assessed so the expected finish is clear before work begins.

Can you remove a stump next to a wall?

It may be possible, but the wall, roots and access need inspection. Mechanical extraction is not suitable for every confined position.

Does stump removal prevent regrowth?

It depends on the species and remaining root material. Some trees can reshoot, so regrowth management should be discussed where relevant.
